I am a cognitive psychologist and vision scientist at Goethe University, Frankfurt. My [research](/research) focusses on our remarkable ability to perceive and make sense of our environment within fractions of a second. I use psychophysics, eye tracking, and modelling to better understand which types of information and cognitive processes enable this feat. 

Besides visual perception, I am generally interested in the many ways our cognitive system "can be wrong", including phenomena such as illusions, memory distortions, and cognitive biases.

I obtained my PhD in psychology under the supervision of [Melissa Võ](https://www.mcn.uni-muenchen.de/members/regular/vo/index.html) in the [Scene Grammar Lab](https://www.scenegrammarlab.com/). As a postdoc at Goethe University Frankfurt, I represent the research unit for Allgemeine Psychologie I (i.e., cognitive psychology). 

I am passionate about [teaching](/teaching) and sharing my enthusiasm for cognitive psychology with students at different levels. I value the exchange with undergraduate and graduate students I supervise and collaborate with.

As an employee of a state-funded university, I am committed to democratic principles, and I am active in several academic committees. In my work, I strive for open, transparent, and reproducible research in the spirit of open science.
